Ortho Flow Recovery Is a Rental Service, Not a Medical Provider

Ortho Flow Recovery is a post-operative recovery equipment rental company. We deliver and retrieve durable medical equipment to support patients recovering from orthopedic and other surgical procedures. We are not a hospital, clinic, physician practice, home health agency, or any other type of licensed healthcare provider.

Nothing on this website constitutes medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a clinical opinion. The information on this site is provided for general informational purposes only, and describes the equipment and logistics services we offer.

Follow Your Physician's Instructions

All decisions about your post-operative care, rehabilitation, and use of recovery equipment must be made in consultation with your treating physician, surgeon, or other licensed healthcare provider. If you have questions about whether any equipment is appropriate for your recovery, ask your care team, not us.

Nothing in our communications, in our forms, or on this site is a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on this website.

Equipment Use

Equipment rented through Ortho Flow Recovery is intended for use as directed by your treating physician or surgical team. We do not prescribe, recommend, or supervise clinical use. Instructions for equipment setup and operation are provided as general guidance only and do not replace guidance from your healthcare provider.

No Claims About Clinical Outcomes

This site does not claim that using our equipment will reduce pain, accelerate healing, reduce the need for medication, or produce any other clinical outcome. Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not contact Ortho Flow Recovery in an emergency. We are not equipped to provide emergency assistance of any kind.
