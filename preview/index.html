Why patients choose Ortho Flow

Cold + compression therapy offers a non-drug approach to managing post-operative pain and swelling. For patients looking to rely less on medication during recovery, NICE1 provides a convenient option that can be used at home.

The Equipment

Cold + Compression for Post-Op Comfort

Pain Management • Swelling Management • Iceless Therapy

The NICE1 system combines precise cold therapy with programmable pneumatic compression to support patients during post-operative recovery. Designed to help manage swelling and discomfort, NICE1 delivers consistent cold therapy without the inconvenience of ice.

Its compact, lightweight design and simple touchscreen controls make cold + compression therapy easier to use at home.

Wraps currently stocked: Foot and Ankle, Knee, Lower Leg, Hip, Lumbar, Cervical, Total Shoulder, Elbow, Hand and Wrist.

Our Story

Built around the part nobody else handles

Ortho Flow was created to bring a higher level of service, convenience, and pain management to post-operative recovery at home. After seeing many innovations in the orthopedic market, we saw an opportunity to better serve patients once they leave the physician's office. By acting as an extension of the care team, Ortho Flow helps patients manage their post-operative needs with personal attention, modern therapy solutions, and service throughout recovery.
