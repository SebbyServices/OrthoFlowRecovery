For Surgeons & Practices

Extending your care directly to patients at home.

Ortho Flow Recovery partners with surgical practices to deliver equipment, hands-on guidance, and ongoing support directly to patients at home, making recovery easier for your patients and your practice.

What OrthoFlow Handles

What we handle for your patients

  • Delivery and setup, Mondays and Thursdays, scheduled around the procedure date.
  • Check-ins during recovery, twice per rental.
  • Pickup once the surgeon clears the patient. No return shipping, nothing for the office to track.
What Your Office Doesn't Have To Manage

What comes off your plate

  • No equipment inventory to stock, store, or maintain.
  • No delivery or pickup logistics for staff to coordinate.
  • No billing conversations. Patients handle payment directly with us.
Referring A Patient

How to refer a patient

1. Recommend Ortho Flow.

Introduce Ortho Flow as a post-operative support option and let the patient know our team will handle the logistics directly.

2. Connect us with the patient.

The surgical scheduler or coordinator can share the patient's contact information with us, or direct the patient to reach out. From there, we handle scheduling and communication.

3. We handle the rest.

Delivery, setup, follow-up, and pickup are managed directly with the patient, keeping the process simple for your staff.

Coverage & Response

Where we serve and how fast we respond

Miami-Dade and Broward counties.

We deliver and pick up throughout Miami-Dade and Broward counties, scheduled around the patient's procedure date.

Responsive support for your team.

We respond to referring practices the same day and work directly with the appropriate member of your team to keep communication simple and the referral process moving.
